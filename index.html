Weekday vs. Weekend — Two Different Restaurants

Weekdays: Commuter-Driven

Worker inflow (43,641) dwarfs the resident base. Demand skews toward quick lunches, grab-and-go, and office-friendly items that a professional crowd can order fast and eat at a desk or in a group.

Weekends: Resident & Game-Day Driven

With most commuters gone, local residents (just 6,462) become the primary segment — alongside local game-day traffic given the venue-adjacent location. Demand shifts to family meals, shareables, and casual dining.

Strategic read: Weekday demand — breakfast, lunch, business meals, and takeaway — is the dominant revenue opportunity in this market. Weekend demand is comparatively small and residency-driven, supplemented by game-day traffic; shareable and bar-food items should lean into a Sports Event Special positioning on game weekends.
